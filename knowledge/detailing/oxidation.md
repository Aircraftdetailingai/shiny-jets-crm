# Exterior Oxidation — Diagnosis & Service Path

**Common ask:** Paint looks dull, chalky, or uneven after wash. Owner says "it used to shine."

## What you're looking at
Oxidation is paint binder breaking down under UV and contaminants. On business jets you'll see it first on horizontal surfaces (top of fuselage, wing upper skins, empennage) and on darker colors.

## Clarify before you recommend
- Age of last polish / ceramic / wax?
- Hangared or ramp-stored?
- Any recent strip / paint / touch-up?
- Chalk transfers to a white towel after a clean wash?
- Clearcoat failure (crazing, peeling) vs. surface dullness?

## Typical service alignment
- Light haze / towel chalk only → multi-step polish (cut → polish → finish) + sealant or ceramic.
- Moderate oxidation → heavier cut compound first, then polish; budget more hours on dark metallic.
- Severe / clearcoat failure → do **not** polish through. Flag for paint shop; detailing can only mask / protect surrounding areas.

## Brett's notes
Never promise "like new" without seeing the jet. Oxidation depth varies panel to panel. Quote a diagnostic polish pass if you're unsure — better than under-quoting a full correction.
