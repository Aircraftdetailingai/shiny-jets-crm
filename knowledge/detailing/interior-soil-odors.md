# Interior Soil, Stains & Odors

**Common ask:** Cabin smells musty, leather looks dull, carpets stained near galley / entry.

## What you're looking at
Business-jet interiors mix leather, Alcantara, wool carpet, veneers, and soft plastics — each needs its own chemistry. Odors often come from HVAC, spills under cushions, or damp carpet pad — not just surface dirt.

## Clarify before you recommend
- Leather type / color; any cracking or dye transfer?
- Odor only in flight / after APU, or sitting in hangar?
- Recent catering spills, pets, smoke, or water intrusion?
- Soft goods last cleaned when? Any prior ozone / enzyme treatments?
- Access: full day cabin empty, or quick-turn while crew waits?

## Typical service alignment
- Maintenance interior → vacuum, surfaces, leather clean/condition, glass, wipe-down.
- Deep interior → above + carpet extraction, detail seams, vent/register wipe, targeted stain work.
- Odor jobs → find source first; enzyme where organic, replace filters if operator allows, ozone only with clear safety protocol and customer OK.
- Damaged leather / veneer → detailing maintains; repair is upholstery / completion center work.

## Brett's notes
Interior oversell without time-on-aircraft is painful. Confirm access hours and whether soft goods can come out. Never soak veneers or flood seats — "wetter is better" ruins cabins.
