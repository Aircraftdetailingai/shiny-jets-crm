# Piaggio P.180 Avanti — Exterior Cleaning note (AMM 12-24-02 / AD context)

- **Source:** FAA AD incorporating Piaggio SB-80-0220 AMM Chapter 12-24-02 Exterior Cleaning—Maintenance Practices (Federal Register); public handling-guide excerpt text mirrored in aviation manual indexes.
- **URLs:** https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2007-12-03/pdf/E7-23343.pdf (AD pointing to AMM 12-24-02); official Piaggio AMM pages remain OEM-controlled.
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-14
- **Applicability:** P.180 Avanti / Avanti II family.
- **Scope:** Detailing-relevant paraphrase only — prefer shop-owned AMM pages 201/202.

---

## Exterior surfaces (public excerpt pattern)
- Mild soap + water; avoid harsh abrasives or alkaline soaps/detergents (scratch paint/plastic or corrode metal).
- Cover areas where solution could damage components.
- Flush loose dirt → soft cloth/sponge/soft brush with cleaning solution → naphtha on soft cloth for stubborn oil/grease → thorough rinse.
- Automotive wax OK; soft cloths/chamois; heavier wax on leading surfaces.

## Windshield / windows
- Remove loose particles with clean water or 50% isopropyl alcohol; remove adhered particles with bare hands before cloth.
- Mild soap + warm water; soft cloth/sponge in **straight** motion; no abrasives/strong acids/bases; rinse; dry soft lint-free cloth.
- **Do not use:** gasoline, alcohol (except as specified dilute IPA for particle removal), benzene, CCl4, thinner, acetone, strong solvents, or window cleaning sprays.
- **Do not use plastic cleaner on heated glass windshields.**
- Thin hard polishing wax after clean; jeweler’s rouge for superficial plastic scratch.
- Rain repellent (e.g. REPCON class) per OEM interval notes in public guides.

## Additional AMM wash themes (easymanuals public summary)
- Ground aircraft; mask sensitive equipment; low-pressure detergent application; extend flaps to clean hidden areas; no brushes on windshields; **high-pressure water prohibited** on bearings/electrical/electronics.

## U-turn
Mild soap + soft cloth straight wipe OK. HP wash on electronics/bearings + listed acrylic solvents = forbid.
