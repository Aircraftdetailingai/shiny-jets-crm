## 7.7 Aircraft washing
- During aircraft washing, the IBF system including the filter assembly should be **protected or removed** to avoid damaging filter media with **high-pressure spray nozzles** or to prevent **solvents from rinsing away the oil** in the filter media.

## Related filter servicing bans (context)
- Do not clean AFS filter with gasoline, solvents, parts cleaners, strong detergents, or caustic solutions.
- Do not steam clean or use HP washers on the filter assembly.

## U-turn
Protect/remove IBF before wash; mild exterior wash per Airbus AMM/SPM when available. HP spray on oiled filter / solvent rinse of filter oil = **forbid**.

## Hold harmless
Advisory only. Prefer current Airbus Master Servicing Manual / AMM cleaning chapters when shop-owned.
