## Windows — clean and polish plastic panels (AMM Ch.12 servicing)

CAUTION: Extreme care must be taken when cleaning and polishing transparent panels; harsh or unsuitable agents can cause damage and crazing. Effect all polishing by hand; frictional heat from mechanical polishers induces surface crazing.

Wash transparent panels with a ~1% solution of Teepol in water, applied with a soft cloth, then polish with approved plastic polish (e.g. Jove Aeropal HR/1 per period data). Other cleaners/polishes may prove satisfactory if suitable; polishes must conform to applicable specs (DTD770 referenced historically).

### Agents / vapours that effect crazing or dissolution of transparent plastic panels (explicit OEM list)

Acetone; glacial acetic acid; ammonia vapour; methyl alcohol; amyl acetate; methylene dichloride; aviation gasoline; nicotine; aviation turbine fuel; rust remover; benzine; butylacetate; synthetic finishes; Bostik; tautening dopes; carbon tetrachloride; trichlorethylene cleaning agents; cresol; Deoxidine materials; ethylene dichloride.

**U-turn note:** Soft cloth + mild detergent solution is recommended. Microfiber is not listed as prohibited; if grit-free and suitable for acrylic, it is a modern soft-cloth equivalent — still avoid every chemical on the crazing list.

## De-ice / frost / salt contamination (AMM temporary revision / Ch.12)

- Do **not** use hot air blasts near windows; take extreme care against overheating painted surfaces, rubber, glass, acrylic/glass fibre, hydraulic lines, grease/oiled surfaces.
- De-icing/anti-icing fluid must **not** contact landing gear electrical plugs/harnesses or brake units.
- Remove salt/chemical melting agents ASAP with clean water; small amounts of wetting agent/detergent (e.g. Teepol / Comprex A class) may be added.
- **CAUTION: Do not use high pressure water** — damage may result to electrical equipment and lubricated components.
