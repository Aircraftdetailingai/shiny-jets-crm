# Piper PA-28-181 Archer AFM Section 8.27 — Cleaning

- **Source:** Piper PA-28-181 Archer III Pilot’s Operating Handbook / AFM Section 8 (Handling, Service & Maintenance), §8.27 Cleaning. Public flight-school posting.
- **URL tried:** https://www.foxflying.com/docs/pa-28-181-poh.pdf (also Condor Aero / Salmon Arm PA-28 POHs).
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-14
- **Applicability:** PA-28 Cherokee/Archer family (typical Piper piston Section 8 cleaning language).
- **Scope:** Detailing-relevant excerpts only.

---

## Exterior surfaces
- Wash with **mild soap and water**.
- Harsh abrasives or alkaline soaps/detergents can scratch paint/plastic or corrode metal — avoid.
- Cover damage-sensitive areas.
- Flush loose dirt → soft cloth/sponge/soft bristle brush → stubborn oil/grease with cloth dampened with **naphtha** → rinse thoroughly.
- Any good automotive wax OK; soft cloths or chamois; heavier wax on leading surfaces.

## Windshield and windows
1. Remove loose dirt with clean water.
2. Mild soap + warm water or aircraft plastic cleaner; soft cloth/sponge **straight back-and-forth**; do not rub harshly.
3. Oil/grease: cloth moistened with **kerosene**.
4. After cleaning plastic: thin coat of hard polishing wax.

### Explicit CAUTION — do not use on plastic
Gasoline, alcohol, benzene, carbon tetrachloride, thinner, acetone, or window cleaning sprays.

## Engine compartment / landing gear (detailer awareness)
- Tape magneto vents before solvent; do not spray solvent into alternator, vacuum pump, starter, or air intakes; do not run engine until solvent evaporated.
- Cover wheel/brake before gear solvent cleaning; re-lube per Lubrication Chart after.

## U-turn
Mild soap / soft cloth / chamois recommended → clean microfiber + aviation wash soap OK if not banned. Listed solvents on acrylic = **forbid**.
