# Pilatus PC-12 Ground Servicing Guide — Exterior / Windows Clean

- **Source:** Pilatus Aircraft Ltd — PC-12 Ground Servicing Guide (GSG) Doc. No. 02527, Issue 001 Rev 02 (public Technical Publications).
- **URL:** https://oldwww.pilatus-aircraft.com/data/tech_pub/67121c060ed3e683893845.pdf (also linked from https://www.pilatus-aircraft.com/en/media/technical-publications )
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-14
- **Applicability:** PC-12 series ground servicing. Advisory vs POH/AFM — POH/AFM prevails on conflict.
- **Scope:** Detailing-relevant cautions/guidelines only.

---

## Windows — Clean and Check (key CAUTIONs)
- Do not put tools/sharp objects on transparencies; remove watches/rings/clothing that can scratch.
- **Do not rub or wipe a dry or dirty windshield/window** — blow/flush/wash dirt first.
- Cleaning materials must be free from dirt/contamination.
- Do not clean in dusty/sandy conditions or strong direct sunlight.
- **Solvents and fuels will damage windows** — do not use solvents or fuel on windows.
- Do not use cleaning agents that are not recommended.

## Exterior — Clean (key CAUTIONs)
- Do not leave tires sitting in soap solution (tire damage).
- **Do not allow heavy-duty cleaner to contact acrylic glasses** (cockpit/cabin windows) — stress crazing risk.
- Use contamination-free water.
- **Do not use high-pressure water** on exterior.
- Flush in airflow direction (nose→tail); do not point hose at vents/openings.
- **Do not let water into pitot probes or static vents.**
- Hot climates: avoid cleaning during hottest hours.

## Exterior — Clean With Water (additional)
- Same HP-water / pitot-static / vent ingress bans.
- Do **not** spray flight controls with water if ambient <32°F (0°C) or aircraft will move into freezing conditions (freeze lock risk).
- Do not spray excessive water on bearings, fittings, control cables, or electrical connectors (lube/CIC removal).

## U-turn
Recommended cleaning agents / soft technique → modern Pilatus-approved or equivalent aviation products OK if not prohibited. HP wash and solvent-on-acrylic = **forbid**.
