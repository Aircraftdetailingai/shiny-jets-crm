## Cleaning topics in 182 SM Section 2
Windshield and windows; painted surfaces; aluminum surfaces; engine compartment; upholstery/interior; propeller; wheels — follow SM procedures for the serial year.

## Typical Cessna SEP PIM cleaning discipline (confirm in serial PIM)
- Painted: mild soap + water; no harsh abrasives; Stoddard sparingly for grease (protect graphics); wax regularly especially salt/coastal; heavier wax on LE/cowl/spinner.
- Acrylic windows: airplane windshield cleaner + soft cloths/flannel; **NEVER** gasoline, benzene, alcohol, acetone, fire-extinguisher fluid, anti-ice fluid, lacquer thinner, glass cleaner; no dry-cloth rub; thin wax OK.
- Isopropyl alcohol for ice on **paint only** — keep off windows.
- Prop: oily cloth wipe; avoid alkaline cleaners (typical PIM language).
