# Cirrus SR22 POH Section 8 — Cleaning Exterior Surfaces

- **Source:** Cirrus Design SR22 Pilot’s Operating Handbook, Section 8 Handling and Servicing (P/N 13772-006 Reissue A example; flight-school public posting).
- **URLs:** https://mikegoulianaviation.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/G6-SR22-POH-Equipped-with-Perspective-Avionics-Reissue-A-2020.pdf ; manufacturer cleaning highlights also summarized at Cirrus “Cleaning Your Cirrus Aircraft” public article.
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-14
- **Applicability:** SR20/SR22/SR22T family (same Section 8 cleaning pattern). Vision Jet — confirm Vision POH separately.
- **Scope:** Detailing-relevant excerpts only.

---

## General exterior
- Cool aircraft in shade before cleaning.
- Mild soap + water; avoid harsh abrasives/alkaline soaps/detergents.
- **Cover static ports** and sensitive areas; remove covers before flight.
- Flush → soft cloth/sponge/soft brush → naphtha for stubborn oil/grease → rinse.
- Silicone-free automotive wax OK; soft cloths/chamois; heavier wax on leading surfaces.

### Ice Protection / TKS (explicit)
- Serials with Ice Protection System: **Do not wax leading-edge porous panels.** See Section 9 supplements / FIKI guidance.

## Exterior windshield and windows (acrylic)
- Rinse dirt before cloth/chamois; **never rub dry acrylic**.
- Solvent-free, non-abrasive, antistatic acrylic cleaner.
- **Do not use:** gasoline, alcohol, benzene, carbon tetrachloride, thinner, acetone, or glass window cleaning sprays.
- Non-abrasive cotton cloth or genuine chamois only — **paper towel/newspaper are abrasive**.
- Wipe **up-and-down** (airflow direction), not circular (glare rings). Fold cloth to clean area each pass.
- Oil/grease: soft cloth + kerosene, then rinse.

## Recommended exterior products (POH table examples)
- Painted: Pure Carnauba / Mothers California Gold / RejeX / WX-Block / AeroShell Flight Jacket Plexicoat.
- Painted + gear: XL-100 Heavy-Duty Cleaner/Degreaser (as listed).
- Engine bay: Stoddard / PD-680 Type II.
- Windows: Kerosene; Klear-To-Land; Prist Plastic and Glass Cleaner; LP Aero Acrylic Polish & Sealant.

## Graphics
- Hand wash preferred; if pressure wash graphics: <2000 psi, <180°F, 40° nozzle, ≥1 ft, nozzle perpendicular — edge-lift risk if angled.

## Interior (brief)
- Prist for interior acrylic; Optimax for displays (no ammonia); mild abrasive-free dishwasher soap for cabin; Cirrus leather care kit P/Ns as listed.

## U-turn
Cotton/chamois recommended → clean microfiber OK if not banned. POH-listed products are recommendations, not exclusive bans on equivalents unless chemistry forbidden. TKS porous-panel no-wax = **forbid wax there**.

## Hold harmless
Advisory only. Match POH revision to serial; Vision SF50 has its own Section 8.
