## Daily / frequent (severe environment appendix themes)
- Thoroughly **wash complete aircraft with fresh water** after flights in salt/severe conditions.
- Clean exhaust deposits off fuselage, struts, belly, HS, elevator with cleaning/polishing wax.
- Wash propeller blades with **noncorrosive soap** solution.
- Clean windshields/windows with cleaning/polishing wax per **AMM chapter 06**.
- Apply protective wax to complete exterior per **AFM Section 8** and AMM ch. 06.
- Water-displacing CPC (e.g. MIL-C-81309 — CorrosionX, ACF-50 class) on interiors as directed — keep off prohibited areas per notice.

## Philosophy
- Keep protective finishes intact; keep drains open; touch up compromised paint promptly.
- Cleaning, lubricating, restoring finishes are primary lifecycle corrosion controls.

## U-turn
Fresh-water wash + noncorrosive soap + AFM/AMM wax path OK. Ignore OEM prohibited CPC zones = **forbid**.

## Hold harmless
Advisory only. Prefer current Daher/Quest AMM + AFM Sec 8 for the serial.
