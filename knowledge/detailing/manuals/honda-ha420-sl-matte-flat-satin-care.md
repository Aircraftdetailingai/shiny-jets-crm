# HondaJet HA-420 SL-420-12-005 — Matte / Flat / Satin Paint Care

- **Source:** Honda Aircraft Company Service Letter SL-420-12-005 Paint Finish – Matte / Flat / Satin – Care and Maintenance (public library mirror).
- **URLs:** https://www.frasersaerospace.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Paint-Finish-Matte-Flat-Satin-Care-and-Maintenance6.pdf
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-14
- **Applicability:** HA-420 S/N 42000011–42000999 painted matte/flat/satin. Gloss finishes use different OEM care — confirm finish type.
- **Scope:** Detailing-relevant excerpts only.

---

## Explicit DO NOT
1. Polish, wax, or buff with rotary/orbital sander or buffer (smooth = counterproductive for matte).
2. Hand buffing, polishing, or aggressive rubbing.
3. Circular motion when wiping with microfiber.
4. Towels with hard tags, abrasive-exposed towels, terry, **paper towels**, or rough towels (swirls/scratches).
5. “Quick detail” sprays/agents designed for **gloss** (waxes/silicones/brighteners → blotchy uneven look).
6. **Dish soaps** (abrasives; drying agents weaken clearcoat → cracking/yellowing).

## Explicit DO
1. Clean microfiber only; not contaminated or dry.
2. Forward/backward **linear** wipe motion.
3. Soap formulated for matte/flat/satin **or** up to 50/50 IPA + water.
   - Method 1: hand wash soap + water + microfiber.
   - Method 2: low-pressure soap apply → low-pressure rinse.
   - Hard stains: soak with ≤50/50 IPA/water before wipe.
4. Optional: CitriCut Concentrate 1:8 (no polymer) — minimal wiping to avoid burnishing.
5. Remove bird droppings, fingerprints, bugs promptly (acids leave permanent marks).

## Notes
- No product restores these non-gloss finishes — prevention is critical.
- Contact Honda Aircraft Technical Support for questions.

## U-turn
Matte-specific soap / IPA-water / linear microfiber = OK path. Wax/polish/buff/dish soap/gloss quick-detail/circular wipe/paper towel = **forbid** on matte/flat/satin.

## Hold harmless
Advisory only. Confirm finish type and current SL revision.
