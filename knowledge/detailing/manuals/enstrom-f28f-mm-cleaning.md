# Enstrom F-28F/280F MM §4-72–4-75 — Cleaning (+ OM transparent plastic)

- **Source:** Enstrom F-28F/280F Series Maintenance Manual Rev 15 (official enstromhelicopter.com); Operator’s Manual transparent-plastic note (Sweet Aviation public posting).
- **URLs:** https://enstromhelicopter.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/F-28F-280F-Series-MM-Rev-15-Complete.pdf ; https://www.sweetaviation.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/F-28F-manual.pdf
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-14
- **Applicability:** F-28F / 280F / 280FX family; F-28A / 480 — verify model MM.
- **Scope:** Detailing-relevant excerpts only.

---

## Exterior (MM 4-73)
- High-quality paint; proper wash/wax for corrosion protection.
- **Do not wash using pressure washing equipment.**
- Secure doors/access panels; flush free of dirt.
- **NOTE:** Avoid direct spraying of main rotor hub, tail rotor assembly, and TR driveshaft bearings (lubricant loss); purge with grease after washing.
- Apply soap with soft cloth in circular motion; flush immediately; repeat until clean; rinse clear water; dry with chamois.
- Wax: good quality paste wax with soft cloth circular; polish with clean dry soft cloth.
- Coastal / high moisture: regular wash/wax; blade tape on MR leading edges recommended when placed in service (see MM §9).

## Interior (MM 4-74)
- **Do not use solvent-type cleaner** on inside of cabin shell.
- Broom/brush + vacuum; quality upholstery cleaner on cushions/floor covering.

## Plexiglas (MM 4-75 / OM)
- OM: fresh water + mild detergent soap; soft fiber tissue; rinse clear; plastic cleaner optional.
- MM: bare hand or clean soft cloth + mild soap/water; rinse; dry soft cloth/tissue; polish with cleaner approved for aircraft transparent plastics.
- **CAUTION:** No coarse abrasive soaps (fine scratches).
- Fine scratches: Meguiar’s Mirror Glaze Plastic Cleaner MGH-17 (as listed in MM).

## U-turn
Mild soap + soft cloth/chamois → aviation wash soap + microfiber OK if not banned. Pressure wash + solvent on cabin shell interior + abrasive soaps = **forbid**.
