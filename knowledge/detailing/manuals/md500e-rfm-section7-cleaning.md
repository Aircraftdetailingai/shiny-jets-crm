# MD Helicopters 500E RFM Section VII — Cleaning

- **Source:** MD 500E (Model 369E) Rotorcraft Flight Manual CSP-E-1, Section VII Handling, Servicing, and Maintenance §§7-11–7-14 (public reprint).
- **URLs:** https://www.heliczech.cz/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/POH-MD500E.pdf
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-14
- **Applicability:** MD 500E; cleaning pattern informative for 369-series / 520N / 530F / 600N family — verify model RFM/HMI.
- **Scope:** Detailing-relevant excerpts only.

## 7-11 Cleaning general
- Oil/dirt: dry-cleaning solvent, commercial-grade kerosene, or detergent soap + water (exceptions below).
- **CAUTION:** Some household cleaners cause corrosion / leave corrosive residue. Examples **not to be used:** “Fantastic” and “409” type cleaners, or locally made strong soap cleaners.

## 7-12 Interior trim / upholstery
- Vacuum/brush floors and metal; sponge upholstery with mild soap + lukewarm water; avoid soaking; wipe with soft cloth dampened with clean water.
- Grease/dirt: upholstery solvent suited to fabric. Dry-cleaning may require re-flameproofing (FAR 27).

## 7-13 Exterior and rotor blades
- **CAUTION:** Prevent scratching aluminum skin on main rotor blades. **Never** volatile solvents or abrasive materials on blades. **Never** apply bending loads to blades or tabs during cleaning.
- Wash exterior including fiberglass/composites and rotor blades with **clean water + mild soap**.
- **NOTE:** Avoid directing soapy/clean water toward **engine air intake** and **instrument static ports**.
- Fuel/oil stains: soft cloth + solvent, then mild soap wash; rinse; dry soft cloth.

## 7-14 Canopy and door transparent plastic
- Outside: rinse clean water; rub lightly with **palm of hand**.
- Mild soap solution or aircraft plastic cleaner for oil spots.
- **CAUTION:** **Never dry plastic panels with cloth** (abrasive grit scratches; dry wipe builds static that attracts dust).
- After dirt removed: rinse; air dry or soft damp chamois.
- Inside: aircraft plastic cleaner + tissue-quality paper towels.

## U-turn
Mild soap + palm/chamois → clean microfiber OK if not banned. Fantastic/409 / strong household soap / dry-wipe acrylic / blade solvents = **forbid**.

## Hold harmless
Advisory only. Prefer current MD HMI for maintenance-level procedures.
