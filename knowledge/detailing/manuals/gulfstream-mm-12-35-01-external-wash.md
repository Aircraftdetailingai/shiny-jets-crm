## Pressure washing
- **Pressure washing is not recommended** — high-velocity water/agents force contamination into bearings, bushings, actuator seals, electricals, faying surfaces, structural joints.

## Preparation
- Safe ground maintenance procedure.
- **Lubricate MLG and NLG before wash** (moisture dam; purge contamination).
- References: windshield surface-seal cleaning 56-11-01; cabin window cleaning 56-21-01.

## Procedure CAUTIONs (explicit)
- Avoid fluids with **chlorides** around engine and APU intakes.
- **Formula 409 is NOT authorized** on Gulfstream (corrosive properties).
- **Do not use brushes** on cockpit/cabin windows — only clean, soft, lint-free cloth.
- Even modest hose pressure at close range can remove joint lubricants / damage components.
- Wash with pH-neutral or slightly alkaline cleaner (car-wash type suggested).
- Manual wash with nylon brush preferred; **minimize water pressure**.
- Keep nozzle **≥3 feet** from surface where possible; stream ≤45° to surface.
- Avoid direct rinse of bearings, bushings, electrical connectors/components; care around gear joints; avoid spray into openings.

## Follow-on (maintenance interface)
- FOD inspect; **immediately re-lubricate** MLG/NLG; apply CIC to MLG spindle pin threads/nuts as referenced; document per authority.

## U-turn
Car-wash-type / soft lint-free cloth recommended → aviation-safe pH-neutral soaps and clean microfiber OK if not banned. Formula 409, HP wash, brushes on windows = **forbid**.
