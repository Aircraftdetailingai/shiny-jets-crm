## 7.7 Aircraft washing
During aircraft washing, the IBF and IVF systems, including the filter assembly, **should be protected or removed** to avoid:
- Damaging filter media with **high-pressure spray nozzles**, or
- Solvents rinsing away oil in the filter media.
