# FAA InFO 16005 — Automotive Detailing Companies Working on Aircraft

- **Source:** FAA InFO16005 (AFS-200 / OPR AFS-330).
- **URL:** https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/other_visit/aviation_industry/airline_operators/airline_safety/InFO16005.pdf
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-14
- **Applicability:** All operators/shops using wash/detail vendors on certificated aircraft.

---

## Core points
- Physical act of washing alone is usually **neither** maintenance nor preventive maintenance (FAA Guidance Alert 8/15/2006).
- But manufacturer CPCP / wash programs usually include **prep and follow-on** that **are** maintenance/preventive maintenance under 14 CFR:
  - Wash preparation (e.g., covering static ports)
  - Post-wash inspections
  - Lubrication
  - Preservation
  - Replacement of defective environmental gaskets/sealant
- Therefore 14 CFR applies to: certificated/trained authorized personnel for those tasks; required inspections associated with washing; documentation.
- Reference manufacturer manuals and current **AC 43-4** (Corrosion Control).

## Detailing AI implication
When advising shops: separate pure cosmetic wash from regulated prep/lube/inspect/document steps; route regulated steps to A&P/IA / operator program.
