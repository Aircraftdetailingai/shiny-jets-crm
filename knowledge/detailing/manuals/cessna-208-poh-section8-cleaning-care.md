# Cessna 208/208B Caravan POH Section 8 — Cleaning and Care

- **Source:** Cessna Model 208B G1000 Pilot’s Operating Handbook / PIM, Section 8 Handling, Service, and Maintenance — Cleaning and Care (208BPHBUS-00 example; public aeroelectric posting).
- **URLs:** http://aeroelectric.com/Reference_Docs/Cessna/cessna-poh/Cessna_208_C208B-G1000_Grandcaravan_POH-PIM_2008.pdf
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-14
- **Applicability:** Caravan 208/208B family (verify serial POH/PIM).
- **Scope:** Detailing-relevant excerpts only (not full engine wash AMM).

## Windshield and windows (cast acrylic)
- Cool aircraft in shade before cleaning.
- Flood with clean water; use **bare hands (no jewelry)** to feel/dislodge grit.
- Mild soap/detergent (e.g. dishwashing liquid) in water; bare hand for rubbing force (clean cloth only to transfer soap — extreme care).
- Stubborn soils on acrylic: Type II aliphatic naphtha on soft clean cloth; refold frequently.
- Rinse thoroughly; dry with clean cloth.
- **Do not use:** methanol, denatured alcohol, gasoline, benzene, xylene, MEK, acetone, carbon tetrachloride, lacquer thinners, commercial/household window cleaning sprays.
- Hard polishing wax on acrylic OK (masks shallow scratches). Polish meeting Fed Spec P-P-560 OK.
- Rain repellent: only products conforming to **MIL-W-6882** (see POH materials chart); keep off painted surrounds.
- **No canvas cover** on windshield unless freezing rain/sleet anticipated (canvas can scratch).

## Painted surfaces
- ~10 days paint cure; polishing/buffing during cure by experienced painter only.
- Wash: water + mild soap → rinse → cloth/chamois dry.
- **Never** harsh/abrasive soaps/detergents.
- Stubborn oil/grease: cloth + **Stoddard solvent**.
- Wax regularly (automotive OK per manufacturer); more frequent wash/wax in salt/coastal ops; seal around rivet heads and skin laps.
- Heavier wax on LE wings/tail, cowl nose cap, spinner.
- Re-wax after soap cleaning or chemical deice.
- Ice removal: isopropyl alcohol OK on paint; **keep IPA away from windshield/windows** (attacks plastic / crazing).

## Stabilizer abrasion boots
- Keep clean; oil/grease swell rubber.
- Mild soap and water; Form Tech AC cleaner or naphtha for stubborn grease; **do not scrub**; wipe solvent before it dries.

## Propeller
- Oil-dampened cloth for grass/bugs; automotive paste wax helps corrosion resistance.

## Engine exterior/compartment
- Suitable solvent / spray cleaner with protection for sensitive components; lubricate per MM after. Compressor wash = P&WC procedure (MX — not detailing soap wash).

## U-turn
Mild soap + soft cloth → aviation wash soap + microfiber OK if not banned. Listed solvents on acrylic / IPA on windows / household glass cleaner = **forbid**.

## Hold harmless
Advisory only. Match POH to serial; TKS/FIKI supplements may add porous-panel rules.
