# Cirrus Vision SF50 — Cleaning highlights (Owners QRG + Cirrus Sec 8 pattern)

- **Source:** Cirrus Vision Jet Owners and Pilots Quick Reference Guide (public Sprinkle CDN); Cirrus “Cleaning Your Cirrus Aircraft” manufacturer highlights pointing to Vision Jet POH Section 8.
- **URLs:** https://cdn.sprinkle.com/library/pdfs/4a51fa1f-0932-4a58-aa22-a333d5ae770e.pdf ; https://50skyshades.com/news/manufacturer/cleaning-your-cirrus-aircraft
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-14
- **Applicability:** SF50 Vision Jet; approved product list lives in **POH Section 8** — verify serial POH.
- **Scope:** Detailing-relevant excerpts only (not full copyrighted POH dump).

---

## Windshield / windows
- **Never** paper towels on windshield; **never** clean dry.
- Clean microfiber + Prist™ or other product on Cirrus approved list (POH Sec 8).
- If cloth drops on ground — **do not reuse** (grit → scratches/swirls).
- Liberally damp surface; wipe **straight up-and-down**, never circular.
- Never chemicals on PFD/MFD screens except as POH allows (Optimax pattern on SR/Vision family).

## FIKI / TKS
- Titanium leading-edge panels with dense porous holes.
- **DO NOT USE ANY WAX ON THE LEADING EDGE** (clogs perforations). Refer to POH for TKS panel cleaning.

## Alignment with SR20/22 Sec 8 family
- Mild soap exterior; cover static ports; silicone-free wax OK on painted (except TKS panels).
- Acrylic bans: gasoline, alcohol, benzene, CCl4, thinner, acetone, glass cleaner sprays.

## U-turn
Approved acrylic cleaner + microfiber linear wipe OK. Paper towel / dry wipe / wax on TKS LE = **forbid**.
