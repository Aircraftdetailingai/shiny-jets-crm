## Guidance
- Air Tractor / NAAA materials stress cleaning the airplane and engine **much more often than annual** — ag chemical / exhaust / hopper residue drive corrosion and inspection quality.
- Removable fuselage skins exist partly for maintenance access and cleaning.
- Fire Boss / saltwater scooping ops (when float-equipped): industry float SM practice is **daily fresh-water rinse** of floats and airframe after saltwater work (confirm Wipaire SM for float serial).
- Prefer `type-class/agricultural.md` + FAA AC 43-4B until AT Owner’s Manual wash chapter is shop-owned.

## U-turn
Frequent fresh-water / mild ag-safe wash OK. Do not invent AT-specific solvent bans without Owner’s Manual.
