# FAA AC 43-4B — Corrosion Control: Cleaning / Washing excerpts

- **Source:** FAA Advisory Circular AC 43-4B, Corrosion Control for Aircraft (9/11/18).
- **URL:** https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_43-4B.pdf
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-14
- **Applicability:** All aircraft (OEM program takes precedence when published).
- **Scope:** Detailing-relevant cleaning/washing excerpts only.

---

## Cleaning compounds (4.13.2)
- **More is not always better** — over-concentrating cleaner raises pH and can do more harm than good. Mix per manufacturer recommendations.
- Use only compounds conforming to government/industry specs or specifically recommended by the aircraft manufacturer.

## Steam cleaning (explicit)
- **Steam cleaning is not recommended for general use on aircraft** — erodes paint, crazes plastic, debonds adhesives, damages electrical insulation, drives lubrication out of bearings.

## Cleaning procedures (recommended)
- Remove/disconnect electrical power; ground aircraft; protect pitot/static and openings as required; wash per approved materials; etc. (see full AC).

## Post-cleaning (recommended)
- Remove covers/plugs/masking; inspect/clear drain holes; inspect water-trap areas; **lubricate per applicable MM**; apply operational preservation.
