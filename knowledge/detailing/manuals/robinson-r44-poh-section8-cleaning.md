## Cleaning exterior including rotor blades

- Wash with **mild soap and water**.
- **Do not use** harsh abrasives, alkaline soaps, or detergents (scratch paint/plastic; metal corrosion risk).
- Cover areas where cleaning solution could cause damage.
- Procedure: rinse loose dirt/salt → apply solution with soft cloth, sponge, or soft bristle brush → stubborn oil/grease with cloth dampened with **aliphatic naphtha** → rinse thoroughly.
- Apply **carnauba wax** to rotor blades; renew when water no longer beads. Any good automotive wax OK on other painted surfaces.
- Soft cleaning cloths or **chamois** when cleaning/polishing.

### Explicit prohibitions (CAUTION)
- **Never use high-pressure spray** to clean helicopter.
- **Never blow compressed air** into main or tail rotor blade tip drain holes.

## Cleaning windshield and windows

1. Remove loose dirt with clean water.
2. Wash with mild soap and warm water **or** aircraft plastic cleaner; soft cloth/sponge in **straight back-and-forth** motion; do not rub harshly.
3. Oil/grease: cloth moistened with **isopropyl alcohol (rubbing alcohol)** or aliphatic naphtha.
4. After cleaning plastic: thin coat of hard polishing wax; rub lightly with soft cloth; **do not use circular motion**.
5. Acrylic (standard windshield): scratches may be removed with jeweler’s rouge then commercial plastic polish (figure-eight). Impact-resistant polycarbonate hardcoat windshields **cannot be polished**.

### Explicit prohibitions (CAUTION)
- Do **not** use gasoline, other alcohols, benzene, carbon tetrachloride, thinner, acetone, or window (glass) cleaning sprays.
- Windshield must remain hydrophobic for rain visibility — verify water beads after any new cleaner/polish.

## Upholstery
Vacuum/brush; damp wipe and dry. Non-leather soiled upholstery: suitable upholstery cleaner; avoid soaking/harsh rubbing.

## U-turn
Soft cloth / chamois / mild soap recommended → clean microfiber and modern aviation-safe wash soap OK if chemistry not banned. High-pressure spray and listed solvents on plastic = **do not use**.
