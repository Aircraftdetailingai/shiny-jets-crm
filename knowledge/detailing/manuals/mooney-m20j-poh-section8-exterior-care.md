# Mooney M20J POH Section VIII — Exterior / Interior Care

- **Source:** Mooney M20J Pilot’s Operating Handbook, Section VIII Handling, Service & Maintenance — Exterior Care / Interior Care (Rev B example; public posting).
- **URLs:** https://www.mattbeyer.com/poh/Mooney-M20J-POH.pdf
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-14
- **Applicability:** M20J/201; M20K/231 family cleaning pattern similar — verify serial POH.
- **Scope:** Detailing-relevant excerpts only.

## Exterior care
- New paint: **DO NOT APPLY WAX** until 2–3 months after delivery (paint curing). Wash to keep dirt out of curing paint; minimize buffing until cured.
- Before wash: cover brake discs; pitot cover on; **mask all static-air buttons**.
- Grease/oil: cotton cloth + **kerosene**.
- Flush loose dirt/mud; wash with aircraft-type washing compound in warm water.
- Soft cloths/chamois; **USE ONLY MILD LIQUID DETERGENTS** — no harsh/abrasive detergents.
- All compounds and cloths must be **free of abrasives/grit**.
- Heavy oxidation: prewax cleaner; then urethane-enamel exterior finish wax per manufacturer.
- Heavier wax on leading edges (wings, empennage, nose) to reduce abrasion.
- Fuel/hydraulic/dye stains: wash immediately. Spilled battery acid: flush, treat with baking soda solution, then mild detergent wash.

## Windows / windshield
- Flush exterior with clear water **before** wiping (remove grit).
- **Do not use household window cleaners** (abrasives/solvents can harm Plexiglas).
- Anti-static Plexiglas cleaner OK for cleaning/polishing.

## Interior (brief)
- Vacuum; mild soap on leather/vinyl; damp wipe; dry soft cloth.
- **NEVER APPLY FURNITURE POLISHES.**
- Foam shampoos / spray dry cleaners for stains; grease on fabric with approved cleaner.

## Propeller (related)
- Occasional oily cloth for grass/bugs; **NEVER USE AN ALKALINE CLEANER ON THE BLADES**.

## U-turn
Mild liquid detergent / soft cloth/chamois → modern aviation wash soap + clean microfiber OK if not banned. Household glass cleaner + alkaline prop cleaners = **forbid**. New-paint no-wax window = **forbid wax until cured**.
