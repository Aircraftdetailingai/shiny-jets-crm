# Pilatus PC-24 Ground Servicing Guide — Exterior / Windows Clean

- **Source:** Pilatus PC-24 Ground Servicing Guide Doc. No. 02484 (official Technical Publications).
- **URLs:** https://www.pilatus-aircraft.com/assets/files/Technical-Publications/Ground-Servicing-Guide_PC-24.pdf
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-14
- **Applicability:** PC-24.
- **Scope:** Detailing-relevant excerpts only.

---

## Exterior — Clean (key cautions)
- **Do not use high-pressure water** on external surfaces.
- Flush in airflow direction (nose→tail); **do not point hose at vents/openings**.
- Water free of contamination; recommended chloride <250 mg/l; pH 6.5–8.5.
- Do not leave tires in soap solution long.
- Do not scratch wing LE; **do not clean wing LE with soft bristled brush**.
- Protect pitot-static probes (no water into probes).
- Hot climates: avoid hottest hours of day.

## Prepare
- Flaps 33°; install covers: engines, ECS intake, VCS outlets, ice detectors, AoA, pitot-static, OAT, nose covers, Battery 2 vent; close panels/doors.

## Wash method
- Flush with clean water.
- Apply soap (e.g. Aero-Klene No. 1002 listed) to ~4–5 m² areas; soft bristled brush; flush; max 3 cycles.
- Flush tires; move to dry area if needed.
- Wing LE: lint-free cloth + cleaning solvent (e.g. Diestone DLS listed); rub along LE curve; change cloths; dry before solvent dries; polish optional (Nushine II S listed).
- Chemical agents: dilute degreasers per GSG ratios; spray + lint-free wipe.

## Wash intervals (Table 2-8-1 summary)
- Severe env: 1–2 weeks; moderate: 1–2 months; mild: 3–6 months (hangared vs not).

## After wash
- Inspect CPC; clear drain holes; open spoilers as directed; contact service centre if corrosion/damaged CPC.
- Remove all covers; flaps 0°; close spoilers.

## Exterior — Clean with water (rinse-only procedure)
- Same HP / vents / pitot cautions.
- **Do not spray flight controls with water if ambient <32°F / 0°C** (freeze risk).
- Do not overspray bearings, fittings, cables, electrical connectors (strips lube/CPC).
- Not a substitute for full exterior clean procedure.

## Windows
- Remove watches/rings/metal objects.
- **Do not rub/wipe dry or dirty** windows — blow/flush/wash dirt first.
- Clean sponges/chamois only; no dusty/sandy conditions; no strong direct sun.
- **Do not use pressurized water** on windows.
- Use only listed cleaning agents (unlisted can damage acrylic/seals/rain-repellent).
- Cockpit outer glass + antistatic/rain-repellent; inner acrylic; cabin acrylic both sides — follow GSG surface-specific steps.

## U-turn
Listed soap + soft brush + lint-free cloth OK path. HP wash / hose into vents/pitot / brush on LE / unlisted window chemistry = **forbid**.

## Hold harmless
Advisory only. Follow current GSG revision and AMM for CPC repair.
