### Key explicit points
- Mild soap + water; no harsh abrasives/alkaline soaps/detergents.
- **Never use high-pressure spray**; never blow compressed air into blade tip drains.
- Acrylic/plastic windows: no gasoline, benzene, carbon tet, thinner, acetone, glass cleaner sprays (same CAUTION family).
- Soft cloths/chamois; carnauba on blades.
