## 3. Compounds, polishes, pads, tools (paint)
**Products (exact names):**
- "Fly Shiny Pro Cut" (not yet released — do not recommend): compound. Best on a DA with a wool cutting pad. It also works on a rotary, but other products give less dust and longer working time on a rotary. [L907-909]
  - ⚠ Book-era pairing. Current single-stage correction → Brett's Shiny Jets method (private).
- "Fly Shiny Pro Polish": "ideal for removing oxidation with the rotary buffer"; very long working time. [L911-912] Also recommended for light oxidation [L940].
- "Red Clay" bar: removes overspray and heavy iron. It causes heavy marring, so **compound afterwards**. Clay by hand with clay lube or dry wash. [L922-923]
- Clay bar pad on a random orbital: **flat surfaces only**. Not over rivets, overlapping panels, or across panels (wing to aileron). Use clay lube or dry wash as lubricant. [L916-920]

**Tools and settings:**
- **Rotary buffer (compounding):** 800-1500 RPM range. Use less pressure at higher RPM and move faster. [L887] Spread at 600-800 RPM, then work at 1000-1400. Brett's usual setup: **light pressure, ~1 inch/second tempo, ~1,200 RPM, pad flat.** Sections 16"x16" to 24"x24". Crosshatch pattern (vertical passes with 50% overlap, then horizontal, repeated once). Line the pad up on the backing plate and rake it outside first. [L956-962]
- **Pad rake:** rake wool pads with the rotary spinning above 2000 RPM. [L905]
- **DA polisher:** less heat and a hologram-free finish; follows the rotary. Service every ~6 months. Protect the backing plate hook-and-loop. Use a 10-12 gauge extension cord. Put product **on the pad** (for the rotary it goes on the surface); don't overload. Section ~16"x16". [L891-897, L969-973]
- **Tornador:** cleans DA pads with air after each section. If you use solution, spin it out completely. For wax/sealant foam pads, use **air only (turn off solution injection)**. [L899-901, L1075-1079]
