## 12. SAFETY / FAA / DAMAGE CAUTIONS (flag list)
**Chemical and mix warnings (from the book):**
- ⚠ **Degreasers must not dry on surfaces** (etching or staining). Test in an inconspicuous spot. **Colored degreasers such as the green in Simple Green can stain paint, permanently on fabric aircraft skins.** [L549-551]
- ⚠ Cleaners dry faster than soap. If one dries, re-apply, agitate and rinse. Always remove or neutralize cleaner, because left-on cleaner "will promote corrosion over time." [L698, L760, L762]
- ⚠ Stronger-pH interior cleaners **must be followed with a neutralizing agent**. [L1710]
- ⚠ **MEK**: last resort, coin-sized spots only, chemical-resistant gloves + respirator, patch test. [L1986-1989, L2049]
- ⚠ **Mineral spirits** are highly flammable and toxic if swallowed, so ventilate. Fly Shiny Dissolver is offered as the alternative. [L536] **Solvents can dry out struts and damage seals.** [L537]
- ⚠ **Fly Shiny Pro contains solvents**, so wear gloves, a respirator and eye protection. [L1249-1251] Wear PPE when applying Air Guard. [L1140]
- ⚠ Tornador spraying chemicals means a respirator + face shield. Wrap a towel around the tip to limit fog. Run a fan and open the doors (moisture near electronics). [L1774, L1778-1780, L1816]
- ⚠ Tornador solution injection should be OFF for wax foam pads (liquid changes pad performance). [L1076-1079]
- ⚠ **Silicone soaps** leave residue that stops touch-up paint and coatings from sticking. [L680]
- ⚠ **Agemaster on silver boots stains the rubber.** [L1593] Agemaster drips stain paint and hangar floors. [L1561]
- ⚠ **Fly Shiny Brightwork Revive must not be used on coated aluminum or around weeping edges.** [L611]
- ⚠ **Metal polish on oxidized paint can stain it permanently.** Tape and plastic-sheet before polishing brightwork. [L1305, L1378]
- ⚠ RejeX can be stripped by abrasive cleaners, polishes, compounds and some paint strippers. [L1087]
- ⚠ Wool carpet: stay in a wool-safe pH range, pre-spray below 140°F, patch-test every spotter (Coffee Stain Remover, Citrus Solv, Stain Magic). [L1976, L1982, L1994, L2005, L2017]
- ℹ The book has **no general "never mix X with Y" chemical-incompatibility warnings** (e.g. no bleach or ammonia content). It only gives the specific ratios listed above. **Do not invent mixes.**

**Aircraft systems / FAA:**
- ⚠ **Pitot-static:** cover the pitot tube and **all** static ports. Water or contaminants cause blockages and bad airspeed readings. Tape must leave zero residue. [L563, L720]
- ⚠ **Landing gear:** if there are leaks, broken parts or abnormal wear, **don't clean it** (you'd hide the source). **Don't wipe the strut or actuator** (removing the lubrication damages seals and causes leaks). Don't bend sending-unit wires. **Keep cleaner off tires** (sidewall deterioration) and flush under them. [L579-581, L589-591, L733-735]
- ⚠ **Interior disinfection fogging is not recommended; "The FAA advises against it"** (electronics failure). References: runwaygirlnetwork.com FAA article; NBAA aircraft disinfection page. [L825-828]
- ⚠ Cockpit: don't move switches or controls, avoid red switches, and tell the pilots to check switches before start-up. [L863]
- ⚠ Interior materials are flammability-certified, so avoid products that degrade them. [L1709]
- ⚠ FAA corrosion map (severe/mild salt environments) sets how often to rinse. Use DI/RO water. [L488-498]
- ⚠ Ceramic coating needs the **aircraft manufacturer's approval** (liability). [L1032]
- ⚠ De-ice boots: cracks from vulcanization can stop ice shedding, which is a safety risk. Excess ice retarder streaks across the wing. Don't abrade boots. Protect vortex generators (Citation Mustang). [L1429, L1574, L1546, L781]
- ⚠ Get approval before test patches or anything that changes how the aircraft looks. [L933]
- ⚠ Damage found in a walkaround means stop work and notify the point of contact. [L484, L834]

**Tool and personal safety:**
- ⚠ Rotary: plant both feet, tie hair back, tuck away cords. Go extra carefully on edges. Cover windows and rubber trim from compound. [L960, L963-964]
- ⚠ Heavy compounding can burn through rivets or paint. [L947]
- ⚠ **Brightwork above 150°F becomes brittle.** Use low RPM (800-900). [L1390-1391]
- ⚠ **Acrylic windows craze from heat.** DA at 4.5, keep moving, use larger pads to spread heat. [L1629, L1663, L1666]
- ⚠ On a ladder, keep 3 points of contact. [L1094]
- ⚠ Wet hangar floors are a slip hazard (and you may not be invited back). [L627]
- ⚠ Blow dryer on glued suede headliners can melt the adhesive. [L1801]
- ⚠ Wear gloves, respirator, eye protection and a jumpsuit for metal polishing. Use a dedicated brightwork Tornador. [L1382, L1328]
