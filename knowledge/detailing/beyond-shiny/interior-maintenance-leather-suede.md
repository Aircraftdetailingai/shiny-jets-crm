## 9. Interiors: maintenance cleaning, leather, carpet, stains
**Maintenance clean** [L786-868]: Get a pilot or mechanic to show you how to operate the doors. Walk through and note any damage. Tape surfaces the vacuum hose will touch. Clean tools and shoes. **Don't enter the interior on a day brightwork was polished.** Phase 1: dry wash + multi-purpose, monitor and window towels, working one side to the back and out. Spray onto the towel, pointed away from electrical panels. Lavatory: 3 dedicated towels (clean → disinfectant with its dwell time → dry), then throw them away. Phase 2: vacuum (claw and crevice tool), dusting brush. Cockpit: tell the pilots it was cleaned, **don't move any switches, avoid red switches**.
- **Disinfection: no fogging** ("The FAA advises against it"). Saturate the towel, wait out the label dwell time, wipe dry. [L825-826]

**Leather** [L1713-1891]:
- Light clean: damp utility microfiber, then a dry microfiber. [L1845-1848]
- Standard: **"Scrub Ninja" + "Colourloc Mild Leather cleaner"** (spelled "Colourloc"/"Colourlock"; approved for Boeing and Airbus). Panel by panel, dry right away with a multi-purpose microfiber, swivel the seat. [L1784-1786, L1852-1856]
- Deep: **Magic Eraser + "Colourloc Strong Leather Cleaner"**. Test first. If color comes off onto the eraser, **stop**, because the leather needs re-dye or replacement. [L1861-1863]
- Stitching: horsehair brush + leather cleaner, then the Tornador with leather cleaner in the solution bottle, nozzle about 1" away, towel wrapped around the muzzle, then air only to dry. Seatbelts: mild cleaner + horsehair brush, then Tornador with water, dried completely. Embroidery: gentler, more distance, don't condition it. [L1871-1876, L1882]
- Condition: **"Colourlock Elephant Preserver"** / "Elephant Leather Preserver" on an applicator, then buff with microfiber. [L1880-1881]
- Suede/nubuck: **"COLOURLOCK Aniline and Nubuck Cleaner"** + horsehair brush, then a low-heat blow dryer while you keep brushing (otherwise it dries hard). Watch the heat near glued headliners. [L1791, L1798-1801, L1886]
- Use a fan with the doors open when you use the Tornador (moisture and electronics). [L1815-1817]
