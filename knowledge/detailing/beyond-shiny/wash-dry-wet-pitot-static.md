## 8. Wash process
**Dry wash (waterless)**, order of operations [L565-633]:
1. Pre-wash walkaround: note damage and leaks. If you find damage, **stop and tell the point of contact**. [L483-485, L555-558]
2. Close doors and windows. **Cover the pitot tube and all static ports** (painter's tape only if it leaves zero residue). [L563]
3. **Oil removal first:** "Fly Shiny Dissolver" sprayed on a cotton terry towel (piston aircraft). On jets a dry terry is enough. Also use Dissolver for Skydrol, and wipe dry before it evaporates. You don't need every last bit off, just break it free. [L569, L728]
4. Landing gear (see safety). Heavy exhaust soot: degreaser or carbon remover on a terry, re-wetting it often. [L574]
5. Wash in a systematic loop, top to bottom. On large aircraft start at the tail. Use a pressurized vessel ("Detail Kegs", 2-15 gal, 120-150 psi, kept on air) and the "Dreadnaught Double Twist Pile 20"x40"" towel. Dry before the solution evaporates. [L518-520, L598-600, L628-630]
6. Turboprop/jet soot during the wash: saturate the damp Dreadnaught with dry wash, mist it with degreaser, wipe, then follow with dry wash and dry. [L575, L631]
7. Bugs: "Fly Shiny Bug Blitz", then dry wash on top to extend dwell, and wipe gently. Repeat with slightly more pressure. [L604-605, L632]
8. Windows twice: a dust pass during the wash, then a final pass with a 16"x16" Dreadnaught, wiping with the airflow. [L621-623]
9. QC walk-around. Remove, clean and reinstall covers. Don't flood hangar floors. [L627, L633]
- "Fly Shiny Static Gaurd Dry Wash": surfactants + drying agents + lubricants, anti-static. [L526-529]

**Wet wash** [L635-785]:
- Aviation soap with **no silicone** (it interferes with touch-up paint adhesion). [L680] Use spot-free RO/DI water. [L683-686]
- Coated/waxed paint: rinse → foam → rinse top-down → optional dry-wash drying aid → Dreadnaught dry. [L742-744]
- Good paint with no protection: spray/foam/rinse/dry. If contaminants remain, brush lightly after foaming, then clay and a light polish are needed. [L747-750]
- Pressure washer: keep a distance where your hand could sit in the stream without hurting. Avoid overlapping panels, pitot/static ports, lenses, door seals, opening windows and fuel caps. [L735, L739]
- Carbon remover: spray it on the brush and agitate. Foam over cleaners to slow drying. Rinse before it dries (etching). [L696-699]
