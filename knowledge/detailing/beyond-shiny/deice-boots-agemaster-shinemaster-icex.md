## 7. De-ice boots
- **Prep and seal at least every 6 months.** ICEX II **every 50 flight hours.** [L1429, L1491]
- Most boots are BFGoodrich; use Goodrich Aerospace products. PBS or Real Clean are "typically not used unless the aircraft is no longer under warranty." [L1430]
- **Washing:** don't use cleaners, because they damage the coating. Dry wash only, light pressure, long wipes. [L615-616] In a wet wash, don't pre-rinse coated boots (water spots). Foam the boots first, then the wing, then dry with a Dreadnaught. On unprotected boots, wipe with a disposable terry (the rubber stains microfiber) and don't dry. [L779-780]
- **BFGoodrich black boots:** Prep ("ShineMaster Prep") → **"Agemaster"** → **"Shinemaster"** → **"ICEX II"**; "Aerospace Protectant" between services. [L1435, L1459-1500]
  1. Dry-wash, then tape the paint, leaving the boot and conductive cement exposed. Leave the bottom tape tag folded down so drips fall to the ground. [L1537-1540]
  2. Strip: keep the prep wet **10-15 min** and work it with a **Magic Eraser**, both kept wet. **Don't abrade** (no Scotch-Brite). [L1516-1518, L1545-1548]
  3. Remove: new terry towels from a water bucket until no black residue comes off, then a damp microfiber, then air dry and inspect. [L1552-1555]
  4. Agemaster: do a water-sheeting test first. Re-tape over the conductive cement. Microfiber applicator filled by tilting the bottle (no drips, it stains paint and floors). **3 coats, 10-15 min between coats, then 24-hour cure** in a hangar with no touching. Remove the tape after. [L1560-1563]
  5. Shinemaster: ~24 h after Agemaster. **3 coats, ~10 min between coats.** One continuous pass with no stopping. Too many coats make it brittle, and short intervals cause grey micro-cracks. Tape lenses and stall indicators. [L1485-1486, L1566-1569]
  6. ICEX II: applicator, let it level **~10 min** (5-10 min), then wipe the excess with a multi-purpose microfiber. Excess streaks across the wing in flight. [L1492-1493, L1572-1574]
- **BFGoodrich silver "Eurathane"/urethane boots:** same prep, then **skip Agemaster ("the agemaster will stain the rubber")**. Shinemaster → ICEX II. The prep isn't black, so use the sheeting test to confirm it's gone. Use "Goodrich SILVERboot Polish" / "BFGoodrich Silver polish" for black marks, every 6 months after installation and as needed. [L1441-1444, L1509, L1590-1593]
- **Silver Estane (older):** BFGoodrich Aerospace Protectant only. Put 10-15 sprays on a damp microfiber to remove bugs, then spray the boot section by section and wipe dry. Never leave it to evaporate. [L1449, L1603-1604]
- **Ice Shield boots:** PBS Prep + Magic Eraser (10-15 min wet) → flush with terry → microfiber → **"Ice Shield Ice Retarder"** (level ~10 min, wipe off excess). A new 3-step line was announced around Oct 2023. [L1455-1457, L1607-1615]
- **PBS:** "Pbs Prep" → "Pbs Boot Sealant" (fast-drying gloss). PBS has no ice retarder, so the sealant is the last step. [L1466-1481]
- ⚠ **Citation Mustang:** it has vortex generators **on the de-ice boot**. If one gets knocked off, the whole boot is ruined. [L781]
