## 5. Fly Shiny ceramic system (Fly Shiny Pro / Air Guard)
- **"Fly Shiny Pro"**: ceramic coating made to be applied with a buffer. It's sold as a **10-year ceramic coating program** through certified installers. Steps: polish → prep → two ceramic layers (DA layer, then hand layer) → topcoat. [L1155-1175] (The book calls it "3-step" in one place [L1279] and lists 4 steps in another [L1169-1174].)
- **Prep:** IPA wipe-down with a plush towel ("motherfluffer from autofiber"), then **"Fly Shiny Pro Bond Enhancer"**. The surface must be free of oils, compounds and polish. [L1257-1263]
- **Layer 1 recipe:** DA polisher + **foam interface pad** + **"saver disk applicator from Autofiber"** (applicator sheet, 5" or 6"). Prime with **20-30 drops**. **Speed setting 2**, crosshatch, sections as big as you can reach. The DA levels the coating, so no separate leveling pass. [L1199-1205, L1268]
- **Layer 2:** by hand with the **Saver Microfiber Applicator** (it has a liner, so less waste). Uses less product. Adds build, gloss and laminar flow. [L1233-1246, L1277-1279]
- **Towels:** Ultra Plush Microfiber for removal. Keep used towels **submerged in a water bucket**, because the ceramic cures with oxygen. Wash, and dry on low heat. [L1225-1231, L1150]
- **Topper, "Fly Shiny Air Guard"** (also "Fly Shiny AirGaurd Spray Ceramic", "Fly Shiny Pro Ceramic Topper", "Fly Shiny Spray ceramic topper"):
  - Recipe: edgeless multi-purpose microfiber soaked and wrung damp (not dripping), folded twice, **primed with 10 sprays**, then **1-2 sprays** per later section. Wipe on, then dry right away with a **dry** ultra-plush towel before it evaporates, overlapping sections. [L1139-1140, L1285-1286]
  - "To avoid high spots, it's crucial to complete the section within 4-6 hours" (quoted as written). [L1139] If you get high spots, reapply and wipe. [L1111, L1145]
  - Works best over a ceramic coating. [L1141]
- **Recharge:** apply "Fly Shiny Pro Ceramic Topper" **every six months**. Wash first, then wipe down with **"Fly Shiny Ceramic Prep Spray"**. [L1290-1291]
- Check the product's cure time before flight. Pay attention to exhaust soot and oil areas. [L1148-1149]
