## 6. Brightwork (polished aluminum)
- **Products:** "Fly Shiny Top cut" / "Top Cut Brightwork Polish" (heavy cut: scratches and oxidation) [L1330-1332]; "Fly Shiny Revitilizer" / "Fly Shiny Revitalizer" (light oxidation, removes holograms from heavy compounding) [L1334-1336]; "Fly Shiny Reflection Fine Polish" (finishing, depth and clarity) [L1338-1340]; "Fly Shiny Brightwork Revive" (wash-stage bug and stain removal) [L611]. The book also names "F7, G6, Shiny Jets Cutting Polish", "Nuvite" / "Nuvite S", and "Shiny Jets Finisher". [L1388-1397]
- **Pads:** white **wool compounding pad** (very aggressive, rake often) [L1342-1345]; **blended wool polishing pad** (a good first pad for most jobs) [L1347-1350]; **cotton velour bonnet** on a random orbital for the hologram-free finish [L1359-1362].
- **Prep:** wash first. Tape the brightwork edges, the fuselage joint and the lenses. Plastic-sheet surrounding surfaces and put down a drop cloth. Tape inlets (1" tape on contours, then wider tape). Wear a respirator (full-face preferred), eye protection, gloves and a jumpsuit. [L1379-1382]
- **Cut step:** spread polish over a ~3-foot area (thin near screw heads; apply Nuvite paste with a chip brush). Rotary + wool polishing pad at **800-900 RPM**, side to side, 50% overlap. **Rake before each new application.** Repeat 3-4 times rather than going more aggressive. **Keep brightwork below 150°F (embrittlement).** If the polish won't polish off, either oxidation remains or the RPM is too high. [L1388-1393]
- **Finish step:** random orbital + cotton velour bonnet + finishing polish (Nuvite S or Shiny Jets Finisher). Remove all polish, and repeat 2-3 times if needed. Clean the bonnet with air between sections. [L1397-1398, L1362]
- **Protection: "Granitize X20-18"** (the body text describes it as "XZILON 3" by Granitize Aviation: UV-30, Boeing-approved for brightwork corrosion protection, 16 oz covers ~1600 sq ft, **3 layers recommended**). [L1364-1370]
  - Prep: **90% isopropyl alcohol** + ultra-plush towel folded into 8 sections. Wipe once with the tape on and again after removing it. If the color changes, oxidation remains, so re-polish. [L1408-1410]
  - Apply: saturate the applicator by holding it over the bottle opening (don't pour). Work 2-foot sections and wipe immediately with an ultra-plush towel using short brisk strokes. If it flashes, reapply and wipe. **3 coats total.** [L1414-1420]
- **Washing brightwork:** no brushes on aluminum. Foam the brightwork **before** the wing so soap drips don't stain it. Rinse and dry right away. [L773-774] Dry-wash a light pass first, then use Revive for bugs. [L609-611]
- Use a **separate Tornador only for brightwork** (metal polish contamination). [L1328]
