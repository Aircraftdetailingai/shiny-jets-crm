## 4. Paint protection: wax, sealants, ceramics
- Lifespans in the book: wax/sealant usually **3-6 months** [L1106]; synthetic sealant "up to six months or longer" [L1006]; spray ceramic **3-12 months** [L1026].
- **Ceramic coatings:** "it's crucial to verify if the aircraft manufacturer approves the use of ceramic coatings." If not, you risk liability and may have to compound the coating off. [L1032]
- **"Rejex" / "RejeX" by Corrosion X** (the wax/sealant they currently use; anti-static) [L914, L1084-1088]:
  - Dwell **20 minutes** before wiping off. Multiple coats, **8-hour cure between coats**.
  - Not for porous surfaces, **highly oxidized paint**, or as a filler for fine scratches.
  - It can go over existing wax (shorter protection). Don't wax over RejeX.
- **Waxing method (RejeX):** DA + foam pad. Prime the pad by spreading RejeX over it with a finger, then run a few seconds at low speed. Spread at **speed 4 or 5**, side to side and up and down. Start a 20-minute timer at the first section. For each new section add **~4 pea-sized drops**. Clean the pad with the Tornador (air only) every few panels and keep several pads on hand. Remove with a plush "Mother Fluffer"/"Motherfluffer" towel. Work in a hangar, out of direct sun. [L1090-1103]
- "Tough Guard" is named as an example polymer sealant (no procedure given). [L1040]
- **Spray ceramic, general technique:** damp low-pile microfiber folded twice, primed with ~10 sprays; re-spray every 2-3 sections. [L1024] Don't spray straight onto the panel. [L1121]
