# Agricultural / utility turbine or piston

## Type-class profile (generic)

**Label:** GENERIC — confirm against this aircraft’s manuals.

### Typical notes
- Chemical exposure (fertilizer/pesticides) drives corrosion — rinse ASAP after ag ops
- Often unpressurized; utilitarian paint; hopper residue
- Prop and leading-edge abrasion heavy

### Detailing defaults
Neutralize ag chemicals per OEM; frequent wash; inspect for corrosion at laps/rivets; acrylic or glass — verify.
