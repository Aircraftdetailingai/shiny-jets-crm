# Ceramic Coating Failure — Diagnosis

**Common ask:** "We paid for ceramic and water doesn't bead anymore" or "coating looks patchy / rainbow / peeling."

## What you're looking at
True ceramic failure vs. contaminated / neglected coating are different. Beading alone is a weak test — contamination can kill beading on a healthy coat.

## Clarify before you recommend
- Brand / installer / date of ceramic application?
- Wash chemistry used since (strong alkaline, acidic fallout removers)?
- Hangar vs. ramp; de-ice / runway film exposure?
- High spots, streaking, or rainbow oil-slick look after application?
- Does a panel wipe with proper APC restore beading temporarily?

## Typical paths
- Contaminated but intact → decon wash, clay/iron as appropriate, topper or maintenance spray; reassess beading.
- High spots / uneven cure from bad install → carefully polish affected panels, then re-coat those areas (match product if possible).
- Coating worn through / failing adhesion → strip (chemical or polish removal per product guidance), correct paint as needed, re-ceramic.
- Customer expects lifetime no-maintenance → reset expectations. Ceramic needs wash discipline; it's not armor plating.

## Service alignment
Exterior correction + ceramic reinstall is a package sell. Don't just "add ceramic" on oxidized paint — correct first or the failure story repeats.

## Brett's notes
Document what failed and why in the job notes. Helps the next quote and protects you if a previous shop's install is the real problem. Never claim ceramic replaces manufacturer paint warranties or OEM care specs.
