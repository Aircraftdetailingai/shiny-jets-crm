## Detailing-relevant guidance

## Model-family extract — MD 500E RFM Sec VII (369-series family pattern)

**Source:** `manuals/md500e-rfm-section7-cleaning.md` (fetched 2026-09-14).

### Explicit / strong guidance
- Mild soap + water exterior including blades/composites; avoid intake and static ports.
- Never Fantastic/409-type or strong household soaps.
- Never volatile solvents/abrasives on MR blades; no bending loads on blades/tabs.
- Acrylic: rinse; palm rub; never dry-wipe with cloth; damp chamois/air dry.

### U-turn
Mild soap + palm/chamois OK. 409/Fantastic / dry-wipe acrylic / blade solvents = forbid.

## Transparent plastics — FAA AC 43.13-1B

See `manuals/faa-ac-43-13-1b-transparent-plastics-cleaning-part1.md`. Mild soap + water; no glass cleaner/acetone/alcohol/fuel on acrylic; no dry wiping; thin wax after clean.
