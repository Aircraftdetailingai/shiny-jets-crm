## Model-specific extract — Cessna 172 / Skyhawk PIM Section 8

**Source:** `manuals/cessna-172r-skyhawk-pim-cleaning-care.md` (Skyhawk_PIM.pdf).

### Acrylic windshield/windows
- Airplane windshield cleaner + soft cloths; finish with soft flannel.
- **NEVER:** gasoline, benzene, alcohol, acetone, fire-extinguisher fluid, anti-ice fluid, lacquer thinner, glass cleaner.
- Alternate: Stoddard → mild detergent + water → moist chamois. No dry-cloth rub. Thin wax OK.
- No canvas windshield cover except freezing rain/sleet risk.

### Paint
- Mild soap + water; no harsh abrasives; Stoddard for grease (avoid graphics); wax more in salt environments; heavier wax on LE/cowl/spinner.
- Isopropyl alcohol for ice on **paint only** — keep off windows.

### U-turn
Flannel/soft cloth recommended → clean microfiber allowed if not banned.

## Transparent plastics — FAA AC 43.13-1B

See `manuals/faa-ac-43-13-1b-transparent-plastics-cleaning-part1.md`. Mild soap + water; no glass cleaner/acetone/alcohol/fuel on acrylic; no dry wiping; thin wax after clean.
