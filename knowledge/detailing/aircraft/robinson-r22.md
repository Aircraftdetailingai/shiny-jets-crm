## Detailing-relevant guidance

## Model-specific extract — Robinson POH Section 8 (Cleaning Helicopter)

**Source:** `manuals/robinson-r22-poh-section8-cleaning.md` (official https://www.robinsonheli.com/publications ; fetched 2026-09-14).

### Explicit / strong guidance
- Mild soap + water; soft cloth/sponge/soft brush; chamois for polish.
- Carnauba on rotor blades; renew when water stops beading.
- Acrylic/plastic windows: straight-line wash; thin hard wax after.
- **NEVER high-pressure spray**; never compressed air into blade tip drains.
- **NEVER** gasoline, benzene, carbon tetrachloride, thinner, acetone, or glass cleaner sprays on plastic (see full CAUTION in extract).

### U-turn
Soft cloth/chamois/mild soap → clean microfiber + aviation wash soap OK if not banned. HP spray + listed solvents = forbid.


## Transparent plastics — FAA AC 43.13-1B

See `manuals/faa-ac-43-13-1b-transparent-plastics-cleaning-part1.md`. Mild soap + water; no glass cleaner/acetone/alcohol/fuel on acrylic; no dry wiping; thin wax after clean.
