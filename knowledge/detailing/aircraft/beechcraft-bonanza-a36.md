## Model-specific extract — Beechcraft Communiqué #111 (Exterior Cleaning)

**Source:** `manuals/beechcraft-g58-baron-ext-cleaning-communique-111.md` (G58_Baron_Ext_Cleaning_Scheduled_Service.pdf; Bonanza/Baron MM Ch.12).

### Explicit / strong guidance
- Follow MM Chapter 12.
- **High-pressure washers are not recommended at all** (gear, engines, avionics overspray risk).
- Lubricate gear/hinges/doors **before** wash; re-lube after.
- Prep: flaps down; locks; close openings; clear drains; cover pitot/static/intakes/vents/inverter louvers.
- Mild liquid soap + water + cellulose sponge; no harsh soaps; don’t contaminate pads on ground.
- Wash underside-out / top-center-out; rinse avoiding lube joints; dry with chamois; remove covers; re-check drains.

### U-turn
Mild soap + sponge / chamois = recommendations → modern aviation wash soap and clean microfiber OK if not prohibited. High-pressure wash = do **not** use.

## Transparent plastics — FAA AC 43.13-1B

See `manuals/faa-ac-43-13-1b-transparent-plastics-cleaning-part1.md`. Mild soap + water; no glass cleaner/acetone/alcohol/fuel on acrylic; no dry wiping; thin wax after clean.
