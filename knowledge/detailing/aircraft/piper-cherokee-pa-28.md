## Model-specific extract — Piper PA-28 AFM §8.27 Cleaning

**Source:** `manuals/piper-pa28-afm-section8-cleaning.md` (PA-28-181 public POH; fetched 2026-09-14).

### Explicit / strong guidance
- Mild soap + water exterior; naphtha for stubborn grease; automotive wax OK; heavier on leading surfaces.
- Windows: mild soap/warm water or aircraft plastic cleaner; straight motion; kerosene for oil/grease; thin hard wax after.
- **Do not use** gasoline, alcohol, benzene, carbon tet, thinner, acetone, or window cleaning sprays on plastic.
- Engine/gear solvent cleaning is maintenance-adjacent — tape magnetos; protect brakes; re-lube after.

### U-turn
Mild soap/soft cloth/chamois → microfiber + aviation soap OK if not banned.


## Transparent plastics — FAA AC 43.13-1B

See `manuals/faa-ac-43-13-1b-transparent-plastics-cleaning-part1.md`. Mild soap + water; no glass cleaner/acetone/alcohol/fuel on acrylic; no dry wiping; thin wax after clean.
