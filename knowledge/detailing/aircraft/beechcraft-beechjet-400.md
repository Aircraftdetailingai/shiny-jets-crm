## Detailing-relevant guidance

## Family extract — Hawker / Beechjet-class windows & wash cautions

**Source:** `manuals/hawker-dh125-amm-windows-wash-deice.md` (DH-125 AMM lineage; confirm on this serial’s AMM).

- Hand-only polishing of transparent plastics; mechanical polishers risk crazing from heat.
- Soft cloth + mild detergent; avoid acetone, alcohols, fuels, chlorinated solvents, ammonia vapours, etc.
- **No high-pressure water** into electrical/lubricated areas.
- De-ice fluids away from gear electrical and brakes.

## Transparent plastics — FAA AC 43.13-1B

See `manuals/faa-ac-43-13-1b-transparent-plastics-cleaning-part1.md`. Mild soap + water; no glass cleaner/acetone/alcohol/fuel on acrylic; no dry wiping; thin wax after clean.
