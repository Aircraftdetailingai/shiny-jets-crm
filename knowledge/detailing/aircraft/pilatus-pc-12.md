## Model-specific extract — Pilatus PC-12 Ground Servicing Guide

**Source:** `manuals/pilatus-pc12-gsg-exterior-clean.md` (official GSG 02527; fetched 2026-09-14).

### Explicit CAUTIONs
- No dry/dirty wipe on windows; no solvents/fuel on windows; no unapproved cleaners.
- No high-pressure water; no heavy-duty cleaner on acrylic; contamination-free water; nose→tail flush; protect pitot/static/vents.
- Freezing: do not spray flight controls if <32°F / moving into freeze.
- Minimize water on bearings/fittings/cables/connectors.

### U-turn
Recommended agents → Pilatus-approved modern equivalents OK if not prohibited. HP wash + solvent-on-acrylic = forbid.
