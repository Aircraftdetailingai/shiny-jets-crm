# Acrylic Windows — Haze & Wipe Damage

**Common ask:** Windows look cloudy, swirled, or "wiped wrong." Owner or pilot is unhappy after a wash.

## What you're looking at
Aircraft acrylic (not auto glass) scratches and hazes easily. Wrong products, dry wiping, or abrasive towels create micro-scratches that scatter light — looks like haze.

## Clarify before you recommend
- Is it exterior acrylic only, or cabin plexi too?
- Recent cleaning by FBO / line staff / previous detailer?
- Rain / hard water spots that were scrubbed dry?
- Any polish or "plastic polish" already attempted?
- Depth: surface haze that disappears when wet vs. permanent scratch you still see when wet?

## Typical service alignment
- Surface haze / light wipe marks → acrylic-safe polish system, proper pads, slow speed, plenty of lube.
- Deeper scratch still visible when wet → may need stepped grit acrylic restoration; set expectations and hours high.
- Cracks, crazing, or delamination → stop. Not a detailing fix; send to window / aircraft maintenance specialist.

## Brett's notes
Never use automotive glass cleaner, ammonia, or abrasive all-purpose compounds on acrylic. Train the crew: wet wipe only, acrylic towels dedicated to windows, no shortcuts. Bad window work loses trust faster than dull paint.
