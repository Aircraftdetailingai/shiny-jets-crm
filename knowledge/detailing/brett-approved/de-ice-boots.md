# De-ice boots: Brett-approved method (Oct 3 2026)

Full system (SOP-07, newest source): boot prep, then AgeMaster, then sealant, then ICEX II, then Goodrich Aerospace Protectant Spray or Fly Shiny Quick Turn. Maintenance on boots that are already coated: wipe on Fly Shiny Quick Turn concentrate, then wipe it off with a dry microfiber towel. The boots come out a darker, shinier black, and ice sticks less. Follow the boot manufacturer's and aircraft maintenance manual limits.

Rules:
- Spell it "ICEX II" (always with the II).
- No reapplication interval is given (no 30/60/90 days, no flight-hour or month schedule). Follow the boot manufacturer's and aircraft maintenance manual limits.
- Boot cleaning is the SOP-07 boot prep step. Don't suggest Fly Shiny Aircraft Wash or Bio Degreaser for boot cleaning.
- Only the boot products in SOP-07 and this note. Don't name or substitute any other boot product.
