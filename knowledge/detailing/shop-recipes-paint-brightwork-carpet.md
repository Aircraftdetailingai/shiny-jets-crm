# Brett Shop Recipes — Paint Correction, Brightwork & Carpet Stains

**Source:** Shop standards taught by Brett Berry (Sep 28, 2026). These are the Shiny Jets procedure of record for the products, pads, and machines listed below. When other knowledge files suggest different products or pads for the same job, **these recipes win**.

## 1. Always ask first: single-stage or clearcoat?

Before recommending any paint correction product, pad, or machine, **ask whether the paint is single-stage or clearcoat** (if the detailer hasn't already said). The recipe is different for each. Do not recommend a correction path until this is known.

---

## 2. Single-stage paint — oxidation correction

### Light to moderate oxidation
- **Machine:** DA polisher
- **Pad:** SPTA coarse blue wool pad (5" or 6")
- **Product:** Menzerna 400

### Heavy oxidation
1. **Rotary** with a **Lake Country steamed wool pad**.
2. Then the same light/moderate step: **DA polisher + SPTA coarse blue wool pad (5"/6") + Menzerna 400**.

---

## 3. Clearcoat paint (e.g., Cirrus)

### Standard polish
- **Product:** Fly Shiny Polish Pro
- **Pad:** foam

### More correction needed
1. **SPTA wool pad** with **3D ACA 500**.
2. Finish with **Fly Shiny Polish Pro on foam**.

---

## 4. Brightwork — one-step polish

- **Pad:** Grant's microfiber pad (Harbor Freight)
- **Product:** 4 pea-sized dots of **Fly Shiny Striker** plus **one spray of Fly Shiny Maverick**
- **Result:** One-step that finishes with **no holograms**.

---

## 5. Carpet — grease, ink, or Sharpie stains

- **Product:** Oil Delete

**Steps:**
1. Spray the stain with Oil Delete.
2. Wrap a **cotton terry towel** around a fingertip.
3. Mist the towel.
4. **Press straight down** on the stain (don't scrub).
5. Repeat on a **fresh section of towel** each time until the ink wicks out — typically **30 to 60 presses**.
